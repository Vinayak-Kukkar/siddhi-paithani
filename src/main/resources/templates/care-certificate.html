5 Essential Heirloom Preservation Guidelines
1. Dry Clean Only
Never wash in water or use harsh home detergents. Always hand over to professional dry cleaners experienced with pure silk & real zari.
2. Muslin Cloth Wrapping
Wrap your Paithani in unbleached white cotton or muslin fabric. Never store in plastic covers, which trap moisture and tarnish gold zari.
3. Quarterly Fold Rotation
Refold your saree along different crease lines every 3 to 4 months. Air in a shaded room for 2 hours to prevent permanent thread stress.
4. Reverse Cool Ironing
Iron only on low silk setting from the reverse side using a protective cotton cloth cover. Never iron directly over metallic zari motifs.
5. Dark & Chemical-Free Storage
Keep away from direct sunlight, damp walls, and perfume sprays. Place natural neem leaves or cloves in cupboard corners instead of naphthalene balls.
6. Lifetime Heritage Care
Passed down across generations, handwoven Paithanis bloom with age when given proper aeration and gentle heirloom care.
