Pure Silk Paithani Care & Preservation Guide

Preserve the royal shine, gold zari pallu, and silk thread strength of your Yeola Paithani for generations.

1. Dry Clean Only

Never wash pure silk Paithani sarees in home washing machines or with harsh chemical detergents. Always opt for professional dry cleaning.

2. Muslin Cloth Wrap

Store the saree wrapped in breathable, unbleached white muslin or cotton cloth. Never use plastic covers which trap humidity.

3. Change Folds Regularly

Unfold and refold the saree along different lines every 3 to 4 months. This prevents gold zari threads from weakening along static crease lines.

4. Cool Ironing Care

Iron on low heat setting with a thin cotton cloth overlay on the reverse side. Never iron direct heat on gold zari peacock pallu motifs.

💡 Pro Artisan Tip: Keep neem leaves or clove sachets near saree wardrobe shelves to protect natural silk fibers from insects!
