The 2,000-Year Royalty of Yeola Paithani

Known as the 'Queen of Sarees', the Yeola Silk Paithani is a masterpiece of ancient Satavahana and Peshwa craftsmanship woven with pure silk and tested gold zari.

100% Handloom Certified by Silk Mark Organization of India

The Legacy of Yeola Weavers

Dating back over two millennia to the ancient town of Pratishthana (modern Paithan) and nurtured in Yeola under the patronage of Peshwa rulers in the 18th century, Paithani sarees have adorned Maharani queens and royal dynasties for generations.

Every single Siddhi Paithani saree is hand-woven by master master-artisans in Yeola, Nashik District using traditional wooden handlooms without mechanical intervention.

Each saree takes between 3 weeks to 18 months of continuous handloom weaving!

The Authentic 4-Step Weaving Process

From raw mulberry silk threads to tapestry-woven gold zari peacock pallu motifs.

01

Pure Silk Thread Sorting

High-grade 100% natural Mulberry silk threads are degummed, washed, and hand-dyed using rich, long-lasting natural color pigments.

02

Gold Zari Testing

Fine metallic threads are coated with silver and electroplated with 24K real gold wire to create non-tarnishing, lustrous zari borders.

03

Handloom Setup

Thousands of warp threads are meticulously aligned on traditional wooden looms by artisan families using hand-turned wooden shuttles.

04

Tapestry Pallu Weaving

Artisans weave peacock (Mor) and lotus (Kamal) pallu motifs using a tapestry technique, leaving zero loose float threads on the reverse side.

Symbolic Royal Motifs

Every motif woven into a Paithani saree carries deep cultural blessings and prosperity.

Mor (Peacock)
Symbolizes majesty, graceful beauty, and royal elegance in Maharashtrian weddings.
Kamal (Lotus)
Represents divine purity, spiritual grace, and sacred blessings of Goddess Lakshmi.
Munia (Parrot)
Embodies warmth, romantic affection, and nature's joyful harmony in festive celebrations.
Asavali (Flowering Vines)
Classical Mughal-Maratha court motifs representing eternal prosperity and abundance.
